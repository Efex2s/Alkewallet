## 2024-05-15 - Material Form and Interactive Link Pattern
**Learning:** Using TextInputLayout with TextInputEditText provides better context (floating labels) and usability (password toggle). Interactive TextView links should always have selectableItemBackground, at least 8dp padding, and be explicitly focusable to meet accessibility standards.
**Action:** Apply TextInputLayout for all form inputs and ensure all text-based links have proper touch targets and visual feedback.
