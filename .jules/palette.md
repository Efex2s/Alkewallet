## 2024-05-23 - Improving Home Page scannability and accessibility
**Learning:** Hardcoded strings and distorted text (textScaleX) negatively impact the app's professionalism and accessibility. Color-coding financial transactions (green for income, red for expense) significantly improves data scannability.
**Action:** Replace distorted text with appropriate text sizes and semantic colors. Always move hardcoded strings to 'strings.xml' and fix any missing accents in localized content. Add 'contentDescription' and ripple feedback to interactive elements.
