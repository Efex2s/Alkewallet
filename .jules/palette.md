## 2024-11-20 - [Responsive Splash Timing]
**Learning:** An excessively long 3-second splash screen makes an app feel sluggish and unresponsive from the first touch. Reducing the delay to 1 second satisfies branding presentation requirements while respecting user patience and optimizing perceived performance.
**Action:** Standardize splash timings to 1000ms across all modules to ensure seamless entry into the app.
