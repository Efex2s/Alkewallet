## 2025-05-14 - Splash Screen Optimization & Lint Blockers
**Learning:** For static Android Activities with no view interaction, avoiding ViewBinding in favor of direct inflation reduces object allocation and initialization overhead. Additionally, reducing splash screen delays (e.g., from 3s to 2s) significantly improves the user's perceived Time to Interactive (TTI). Crucially, pre-existing lint errors (like MissingClass in the Manifest) can block build verification of performance changes and must be resolved.
**Action:** Always evaluate if ViewBinding is truly necessary for an Activity. Favor direct inflation for static/simple screens. Keep splash delays at the minimum required for branding. Check for and fix unrelated manifest errors early to unblock the verification pipeline.
