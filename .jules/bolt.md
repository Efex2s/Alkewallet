## 2025-05-30 - Artificial Splash Delays
**Learning:** Found an artificial splash screen delay of 3000ms. These are often added for branding but negatively impact the perceived performance of the app. Reducing them to a more reasonable value (e.g., 1000ms) or removing them if possible improves startup time.
**Action:** Always audit Splash Screen activities for hardcoded delays and suggest reductions to improve UX.
