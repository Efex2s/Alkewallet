## 2024-07-05 - Hardcoded UI Delays as Bottlenecks
**Learning:** Hardcoded delays in entry activities (like Splash Screens) are primary bottlenecks for Time to Interactive (TTI) and perceived performance.
**Action:** Always scrutinize Splash Screens and auto-navigation timers for opportunities to reduce artificial waits.
